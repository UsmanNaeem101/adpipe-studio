# 25 · Terminology

The words they actually use. Your language bank for copy, search terms and page
wording.

## What to capture

The functional vocabulary — what they call the problem, the body part, the
products, the treatments, the sensations.

```
their word              the "correct" word
crick                   cervical strain
knots                   trigger points
trap                    trapezius
pins and needles        paresthesia
went out / went ping    acute onset
```

## Capture theirs, not the right one

The entire point is resonance. If nobody in the corpus says "cervical," your page
should not say "cervical." Record what they type, including how they misspell it —
misspellings are search terms too.

## Note who uses what

Vocabulary is a segmentation signal in its own right. Gym people say "impingement"
and "mobility"; general sufferers say "trapped nerve" and "knot." If a term is
concentrated in one group, say so — you've found a way to speak to them and only
them.

## Give me

Each term, what it refers to, how many distinct people use it, its surface variants
and misspellings, and where it clusters. Then the ten terms I should use verbatim in
copy, and any "correct" terms I should avoid because the audience doesn't use them.
