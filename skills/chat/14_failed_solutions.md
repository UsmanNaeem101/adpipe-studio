# 14 · Failed solutions

What they've already tried that didn't work. Some of the most valuable material in
the whole corpus.

## What one is

An **actual attempt** plus **its outcome**. Both halves required.

```
❌ "I use a foam pillow" (mention → 21)     ✅ foam pillow — made the neck worse
❌ "physio never works" (belief → 12)       ✅ physio — helped, but couldn't keep up the exercises
❌ "might try a chiropractor" (not tried)   ✅ chiropractor — helped but too costly to continue
❌ "tried things" (not specific)            ✅ memory foam pillow — relief faded after two weeks
```

## Record *how* it failed — that's the whole value

There are four different failures and they lead to four different ads:

```
never worked        did nothing from day one
worked then faded   relief that wore off — the most commercially useful
worked but          effective and unsustainable (too costly, too slow, too fiddly)
made it worse       actively harmful
```

"Relief faded after two weeks" is a completely different customer from "did nothing
at all." The first believes the category can work and wants a better version. The
second has to be re-convinced the category works at all.

## Why this is the objection list in advance

Everything here is something a buyer will assume about *you*. If half the segment
has been burned by memory foam going flat, your page has to answer that before it
sells anything. Rank by how many people were burned, and record what they said when
it failed — that phrasing is the objection, already written.
