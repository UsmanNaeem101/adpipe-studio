# 26 · Slang and vivid language

The colourful register — metaphors, nicknames, exaggerations, jokes. Your hook
bank.

Sibling of 25: that one captures the *functional* vocabulary, this one captures
what carries emotion.

## What to capture

```
metaphors      "like someone's got a screwdriver in the joint"
nicknames      "my dodgy shoulder", "the bad side"
exaggeration   "I'd cut the arm off at this point"
community      sub-specific shorthand and in-jokes
gallows humour "my shoulder is 87 years old and I'm 34"
```

## Why this is the highest-leverage language in the corpus

Nobody can write this. A copywriter inventing "like a screwdriver in the joint"
would be pleased with themselves; a customer saying it is proof that's how it
feels. Recognition is what stops a scroll, and you can only get it by stealing the
words.

## Rules

- Verbatim, always, including the swearing.
- Keep the surrounding half-sentence — this language often doesn't survive being cut
  loose from its context.
- Record how many people use a similar image. One person's metaphor is a lucky find;
  four people reaching for the same one is a hook.
- Don't clean up crude phrasing. If they said it, it's data. You can decide later
  what's usable.

context. Then the top ten as ready-to-test hook lines, unaltered.
