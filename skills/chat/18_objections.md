# 18 · Objections

Why they hesitate. The most directly conversion-relevant dimension in the set —
every one of these is something your page has to answer.

## What one is

Resistance **tied to the decision**, with an object.

```
❌ "this pain is ruining my life" (venting)   ✅ sceptical a pillow can fix nerve pain
❌ "brand X is rubbish" (competitor → 22)     ✅ too expensive to risk on something unproven
❌ "doubt" (no object)                        ✅ worried it won't suit a side sleeper
```

## Don't manufacture objections

```
❌ they haven't bought, so "they must object to price"
✅ "what's the return policy?" → an implied risk objection
```

Only record resistance someone actually expressed. A silent non-buyer tells you
nothing, and inventing their reason gives you a page answering an objection nobody
has.

## The recurring shapes

```
scepticism      "I don't believe that would work for my problem"
risk            "what if it doesn't work and I'm stuck with it"
price           "not at that price, not without proof"
fit             "that won't work for someone who sleeps like I do"
effort          "I don't want another thing to keep up with"
been-burned     "I've bought three of these already"
```

That last one is where 14 and 18 meet. A failed solution *becomes* an objection to
the next purchase — cross-reference them.

## Give me, per objection

Who says it, how many, what would answer it if they said, and the verbatim phrasing.
The phrasing matters most: that's the sentence your FAQ has to echo back.
