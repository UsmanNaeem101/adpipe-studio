# 16 · Buying triggers

The event that turned "I have this problem" into "I'm doing something about it
today." The why-now.

## What one is

A **precipitating event** plus **the action it produced**. Both halves.

```
❌ "my neck's ached for years" (ongoing)     ✅ the morning they couldn't turn their head
                                                 → booked a physio that week
❌ "I want to sleep better" (a want → 09)    ✅ a big presentation after three bad nights
                                                 → ordered a pillow overnight
❌ "I bought a pillow" (no precipitant)      ✅ the physio recommending one → bought it that day
❌ "frustrated with it" (feeling, no action) ✅ fed up enough one bad night to order that evening
```

## Every other dimension is a state. This one is a moment in time.

That makes it the most useful thing you'll find for *timing* — when to reach
people, and what to say at the moment they're reachable.

## The common shapes

```
a threshold crossed     "I couldn't lift my kid" — the thing they won't accept losing
an authority said so    physio, GP, friend who had the same thing
a deadline              wedding, holiday, presentation, new job
a scare                 numbness, a noise, a night that felt different
a trigger event         new mattress, new desk, new baby, house move
seeing someone else     a post, a review, a friend's result
```

Record which shape, because each one is a different ad. A deadline trigger wants
urgency and speed of delivery. A scare trigger wants reassurance and authority.
