# 08 · Pain moments

The concrete scenes where the problem becomes real. Where the pain *happens*.

## What one is

A specific, recognisable moment in a day. If you can picture it as a shot in an ad,
it's a moment. If it's a general state, it belongs in 07.

```
❌ neck pain          ✅ turning to check the blind spot while driving
❌ shoulder pain      ✅ pulling a jumper over the head and feeling it catch
❌ poor sleep         ✅ waking after rolling onto the painful shoulder
❌ back pain          ✅ standing up after sitting through a long meeting
```

## Why this one matters commercially

Moments are where hooks come from. "Shoulder pain" sells nothing. "That moment you
reach for the seatbelt" stops the scroll, because the person lives it three times a
day and has never seen it named.

So bias toward the specific and the physical. Time of day, body position, the
object involved, who else is there.

## Keep the trigger and the consequence

A moment has a shape: something happens, the pain fires, something follows.

```
reached up to the top shelf → sharp catch → had to put the plate down
```

Record all three where the comment gives them. The consequence is often the most
useful part — it's what the customer actually resents.

## Don't invent scenes

If someone says "it hurts when I move it," that is not a moment, it's a vague
statement. Don't turn it into "reaching for a mug in the morning" because that
sounds better. Leave it out.
