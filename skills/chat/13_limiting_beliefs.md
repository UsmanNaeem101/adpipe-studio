# 13 · Limiting beliefs

The beliefs that stop people acting. A focused cut of 12 — only the ones that
visibly block behaviour.

## The test: does it change what they do?

```
❌ "memory foam sleeps hot" (belief, no    ✅ "nothing works for me, so I've stopped
    behavioural effect → 12)                   looking"
❌ "I'll check reviews first"              ✅ "reviews are all fake, no point looking"
    (proportionate caution)
❌ "this is permanent" (belief only)       ✅ "it's permanent, so I won't waste money
                                               trying anything else"
```

The pattern is **belief → therefore → inaction**. If the comment doesn't show the
"therefore," it's a belief for 12, not a limiting belief.

## Don't editorialise

```
❌ "defeatist mindset"
✅ record the belief, and the behaviour it produced
```

You're not assessing anyone's psychology. You're noting that a stated belief is
attached to a stated non-action.

## Why these are worth the most

Each one is a specific thing your page has to dismantle before anything else works.
A customer who believes nothing will ever help doesn't read your feature list. Rank
them by how many people hold them and how completely they block action.
