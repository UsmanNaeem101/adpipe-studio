# 12 · Beliefs

What they hold to be true. You're a recorder here, not a fact-checker.

## What one is

A claim about how the world works, stated as fact.

```
❌ "I hate memory foam" (emotion → 10)     ✅ memory foam sleeps too hot to be worth it
❌ "I prefer firm" (preference)            ✅ firmer pillows are always better for the neck
❌ "I use a foam pillow" (mention → 21)    ✅ posture is what causes all of this
```

## Record it as held, even when it's wrong

Especially when it's wrong. A folk theory that half the market believes is worth
more to you than the correct explanation, because it's what your copy has to meet.

```
❌ "cracking necks causes arthritis (WRONG)"
✅ cracking your neck causes arthritis — held by N people
```

Don't annotate, don't correct, don't hedge. If a belief is contradicted elsewhere in
the corpus, record both and note they conflict — a split belief is a segmentation
signal.

## Capture the confidence

"I think it might be posture" and "it's obviously posture" are different data.
Record how firmly it's held: suspected, believed, or treated as settled fact. The
firm ones are what you have to argue against; the soft ones are what you can shape.
