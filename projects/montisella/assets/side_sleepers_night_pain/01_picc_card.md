## Step 1 — Buying barriers, ranked (skill 27)

Synthesised from the extraction dimensions, not re-counted from the corpus.

| # | Barrier | Type | Why it ranks here |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **"I can't tell which one is right for me before I buy it."** | Selection | The most-stated problem is a fit dilemma, not a quality one: *"If it's too low I squish my shoulder and if it's too high my neck will hurt."* [2] Buyers can't self-diagnose the right height, so they guess, fail, and blame the category. |
| 2 | "I've already bought several and none worked." | Trust / burned | *"Spent thousands on every different style over the years."* [64] · *"I tried a bunch of pillows."* [25] · *"I tried the koala cloud, was waaaaaayyy too soft."* [57] Failure is repeat, not first-time — a first-time-buyer pitch misses them entirely. |
| 3 | "It'll feel fine at first and then go flat." | Durability | *"once they are broken in enough, they are head pillows- when they get too flat, they go between my knees"* [3] · *"After it started getting a little flat, I had spare filling and added it back to the pillow."* [132] Flattening is described as inevitable — a category assumption, not a brand complaint. |
| 4 | "It'll be uncomfortable while I get used to it." | Switching cost | *"It took a while for my body to get used to it, a lot of minor propping."* [214] · *"It is really hard to get used to."* [97] They expect a bad adjustment fortnight and price it in. |
| 5 | "A pillow isn't the real fix — this is a body problem." | Category doubt | 163 mentions of stretching, 49 massage, 34 physio, 28 chiro. They run daytime interventions against a problem that happens at night. |

**Primary barrier: #1, selection.** It is the only barrier a static ad can move on its own. #2 and #3 are answered by the offer (trial, warranty), not by copy — and both are currently `NEEDS INPUT` in `facts.json`.

> ⚠️ **Barrier #5 is where the overclaim pressure sits.** The honest answer to "a pillow won't fix my body" is *"no, and we're not claiming it will."* Any version that answers it with correction, realignment, or relief is a claim we cannot substantiate. **Flagged, not answered** — that is a signal the angle is wrong, not a licence.

## Compliance note

This corpus is dense with medical framing — numbness, tingling, radiating pain, nerve talk, physio and chiro referrals — because that is how sufferers speak. **None of it becomes a Montisella claim.**

Two live risks in this segment specifically:

1. **Numbness (39 items) is the most tempting and most dangerous pain to name.** "Wake up without the dead arm" is a symptom-relief claim. The compliant register is the felt night, not the symptom: *waking up without having to shake your arm out* describes what they do, not what we cured.
2. **Barrier #5 cannot be answered compliantly.** Do not build an angle that responds to "a pillow won't fix my body."

Mechanism (19) and proof (20) carry the risk. Deploy the felt mechanism — *height matched to your shoulder, held all night* — never the medical one.

`facts.json` is empty, so **no concept may carry a number**: no trial length, no warranty, no density, no price. `qa.py` will fail any that do.
